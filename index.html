🔄 Re-attribute Supervisors

If DSATs were submitted before you ran a hierarchy import, they may still show the old supervisor. This tool scans every DSAT on or after your chosen date, looks up the correct supervisor using the versioned hierarchy for that specific DSAT date, and shows you exactly what would change before you commit.

⚠️ This only updates Supervisor and Manager fields on the DSAT record. All audit content, DSAT Date, and submission details remain untouched.
All DSATs with a DSAT Date on or after this date will be checked.
